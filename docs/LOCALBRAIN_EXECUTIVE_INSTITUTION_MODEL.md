## The shift

Originally:

> Let's build a really smart AI.

Today:

> Let's build a **sovereign executive institution** where AI is one of many tools.

AI models evolve. Providers come and go. An institution with governance, memory, departments, and operational discipline **outlasts any technology stack**.

Protect: **brain above providers · institution above tools**.

## Every department becomes an office

Not a module — an **organization** with internal structure.

| Office | Executive | Teams (examples) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Finance Office** | CFO | Treasury · Budget · Forecasting · Grants · Accounting · Payroll · Compliance |
| **Communications Office** | Chief of Communications | Email · SMS · Press · Social · Reputation · AI Comms · Branding |
| **Knowledge Office** | CKO | Memory · Provenance · Knowledge · World Model · Learning · Research |
| **Security Office** | CSO | Privacy · Encryption · Identity · Threat Intelligence · Cybersecurity · Compliance |

The executive (CFO, CoS, etc.) leads the **office**, not a single capability.

ENG-EO-001 departments today are seeds of future offices. `lifecycle: reserved` departments mature into full offices as teams and capabilities accumulate.

## Doctrine hierarchy (reserved — do not build yet)

Large organizations don't store every rule in one document.

```txt
Executive Constitution v2
        ↓
Office Constitution (per executive office instance)
        ↓
Department Charters
        ↓
Standing Orders
        ↓
Playbooks
        ↓
Policies
        ↓
Procedures
```

**Why reserve:** Over years the institution accumulates doctrine at multiple levels. One constitution cannot hold everything.

Today: [LOCALBRAIN_CONSTITUTION.md](./LOCALBRAIN_CONSTITUTION.md) + department standing orders in ENG-EO-001.

Future: `Executive Constitution v2` extends binding law; each instantiated office carries an **Office Constitution**; departments retain charters and standing orders already modeled in `executiveOffice.ts`.

**Reserved:** `LB-OS-CON-002` — Office Constitution & multi-level doctrine hierarchy (doctrine only until Convention completes)
