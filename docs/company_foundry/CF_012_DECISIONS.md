# CF-012 Decision Record — Self-Guided Builder Academy

**Date:** 2026-08-28

## CF-DEC-050 — Capstone design is mandatory for graduation
Every learner must complete a Capstone Notebook, full Master Build Plan, Capstone application, and Capstone defense to graduate the Builder Academy.

## CF-DEC-051 — Capstone implementation is competitive
A learner may graduate without the Foundry selecting the Capstone for implementation. Pilot selection is a separate capital-allocation decision.

## CF-DEC-052 — Non-selected graduates can enter phase production
Graduates whose Capstones are not selected may be placed on Company Foundry phase projects according to capability and available productive work.

## CF-DEC-053 — Capstone begins during onboarding
Capstone development starts on Day 1 as an evolving Notebook. Each Academy stage adds a governed checkpoint so product thinking grows alongside technical capability.

## CF-DEC-054 — Real production phases precede Capstone implementation
Before any selected Capstone pilot begins, the learner must complete accepted production phases on existing Foundry assets. The Academy must prove production capability before the company funds a learner-led venture build.

## CF-DEC-055 — Academy is self-guided by design
The standard learning path must not depend on a standing live instructor. The system acts as lesson sequencer, AI tutor, project manager, assessment engine, Capstone coach, and progression gate. Authorized humans remain required for production acceptance, employment/conduct decisions, graduation authority, and capital allocation.

## CF-DEC-056 — Multi-AI orchestration is a core skill
Builders are explicitly trained to use strategic AI, Cursor/repository AI, and independent reviewer AI as complementary roles. Parallel AI work must be divided safely rather than allowing uncontrolled simultaneous edits to the same code surface.

## CF-DEC-057 — Prompting is taught as specification writing
The Foundry does not teach prompt tricks as the core skill. Builders learn to specify mission, context, read-first instructions, scope, boundaries, requirements, acceptance evidence, validation, Git discipline, and return reporting.

## CF-DEC-058 — Foundry fronts approved Capstone Pilot build costs
For a selected Capstone Pilot, Company Foundry may fund the approved build budget, including eligible trainee labor, qualified builder phases, reviewers, compute/tooling, hosting, testing, and approved validation costs.

## CF-DEC-059 — Capstone pilots carry a Foundry Advance Balance
Approved funded costs create a tracked Foundry Advance Balance (FAB). This is a product-level cost-recovery ledger, not ownership.

## CF-DEC-060 — 50% revenue recovery layer precedes normal residual economics
While FAB is above zero, the current founder-doctrine model allocates 50% of defined Eligible Recovery Revenue to Foundry cost recovery and sends the other 50% through the approved product operating waterfall. The legal/accounting definition of Eligible Recovery Revenue must be finalized before production use.

## CF-DEC-061 — Standard residual formula activates after recovery
When FAB reaches zero, the temporary recovery allocation ends. Standard Capstone DPR economics then govern: Company Foundry at least 25%, Capstone lead no more than 51%, named team members receive approved percentages, total 100%.

## CF-DEC-062 — Capstone pilots may recruit team candidates but cannot promise economics independently
Selected pilot leads may help recruit coders/builders for their teams. All recruits still pass Foundry admission, classification, access, compensation, and residual-authorization controls. Recruiting itself does not create economic rights.

## CF-DEC-063 — Curriculum moves from scaffolded prompts to independent product judgment
Early learners receive templates and exact instructions. Scaffolding is progressively removed until the learner can independently identify a problem, inspect a system, create a Master Build Plan, orchestrate AI, estimate a build, recruit a team, and defend the commercial/technical thesis.

## CF-DEC-064 — CF-012 implementation is phased
CF-012A establishes the executable curriculum registry. Subsequent slices build learner progress, Capstone Notebook, production-lab integration, Master Plan Builder, Capstone defense, and pilot funding/recovery ledger.
