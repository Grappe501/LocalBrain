# 1. Why Company Foundry Exists

Company Foundry is designed around a simple observation: artificial intelligence has dramatically reduced the cost and time required to turn ideas into software, research products, books, data systems, and digital businesses, but most people and organizations still lack the method, discipline, architecture, product judgment, and operating system required to turn that capability into durable economic value.

The Foundry is not intended to be a conventional software agency, coding bootcamp, venture studio, or SaaS company. It combines pieces of all four.

Its core business is converting **ideas + existing intellectual property + AI + trained builders + disciplined phase engineering** into revenue-producing products while allowing the company, founders, builders, project leads, authors, and teams to participate in the resulting economics.

The thesis is that development capacity itself can become a compounding asset. Instead of repeatedly hiring expensive conventional development teams, Company Foundry teaches motivated people an AI-native construction method, gives them real bounded production work, measures demonstrated capability, and uses the strongest builders to expand the company's product capacity.

# 2. The Foundry Model

The company operates as three mutually reinforcing businesses.

## Product Company
Company Foundry develops, owns, licenses, operates, or participates economically in software, books, data products, educational products, and other intellectual property.

## Implementation and Build Studio
The company sells paid pilots, implementations, customizations, integrations, and bounded development work. This is expected to be the earliest cash-flow engine because a small number of institutional contracts can generate more near-term revenue than hundreds of low-price SaaS subscriptions.

## Academy and Builder Foundry
The Academy teaches the Company's AI-native build methodology. Learners progress from absolute beginner concepts through prompting, Cursor, Git/GitHub, deployment, product engineering, phase construction, Master Build Plans, production work, and a mandatory Capstone.

The Academy is therefore not separate from production. It is the talent engine that increases the Foundry's productive capacity.

# 3. The Compounding Flywheel

The intended economic flywheel is:

**Existing intellectual property and ideas**
→ prioritize commercially credible products
→ sell pilots / implementations / subscriptions
→ generate cash
→ finance controlled trainee hours
→ teach builders on real bounded work
→ increase development capacity
→ graduate demonstrated builders
→ evaluate Capstone Master Plans
→ selectively finance credible Capstone pilots
→ launch additional products
→ recover Foundry advances from defined revenue
→ activate approved post-recovery residual economics
→ retain Company participation in successful products
→ finance more builders and more products.

The central measurement is not the number of products created. It is whether **one dollar entering the Foundry can reproduce itself while increasing productive capacity and intellectual property**.

# 4. Founder Build Method

The methodology being institutionalized is an AI-native construction discipline rather than unrestricted 'vibe coding.' Core behaviors include:

1. Start with the human problem and desired outcome.
2. Engineer the concept before writing large amounts of code.
3. Create a Master Build Plan that preserves founder intent.
4. Divide large ambitions into bounded, independently acceptable phases.
5. Use multiple AI systems strategically for reasoning, research, review, and construction.
6. Use Cursor or equivalent repository-aware agents for bounded implementation.
7. Inspect before modifying.
8. Treat the diff and repository as truth rather than an AI completion claim.
9. Validate every meaningful phase with appropriate tests/builds/evidence.
10. Commit and preserve progress systematically through Git/GitHub.
11. Deploy through controlled infrastructure such as Netlify when appropriate.
12. Maintain human approval for consequential decisions.
13. Report what changed, what passed, what remains uncertain, and what should happen next.

The Academy exists to make this method transferable rather than dependent on a single founder.

# 7. Cohort System

The Academy is intended to accept motivated people who may not be conventional developers. Selection emphasizes learning capacity, reliability, judgment, team behavior, persistence, and alignment with the Foundry method.

## Training Compensation
The current working model is **$20 per hour while interning/training**, subject to employment classification, wage-and-hour, tax, payroll, and legal review. Training labor is controlled by cohort liquidity and approved project budgets.

## Progression
Learners move through:

**Foundations → AI collaboration → Cursor/repository work → GitHub/deployment → concept engineering → phase production → Master Build Plans → Capstone defense.**

They begin thinking about the Capstone early. The Capstone develops alongside their education rather than appearing as a final surprise.

## Real Production
As capability increases, learners can receive bounded Foundry production phases. Work is accepted based on evidence and independent review where required. AI cannot certify its own work.

## Graduation
Every learner must complete the Capstone process to graduate. Graduation and Capstone pilot selection are deliberately separate decisions.

A person may graduate because they have demonstrated the Foundry method while the company declines to finance their Capstone. That graduate can continue on Foundry phase teams.

# 8. Capstone Venture System

The Capstone is a Master Plan-backed product proposal and demonstration of builder judgment.

The pathway is:

**Capstone Notebook → engineered concept → Master Build Plan → independent red team → application → defense → graduation decision → separate pilot-selection decision.**

Capstones are evaluated for market, technical, economic, and execution viability.

Accepted pilot status does **not** itself authorize money. Funding requires a separate authorization and approved budget.

The Foundry can finance selected pilots because doing so creates a pathway from training into new company economic assets without promising that every learner becomes a founder or every idea receives capital.

# 9. Foundry Advance Balance and Recovery

For a selected and separately authorized Capstone pilot, approved Foundry-funded development costs create a **Foundry Advance Balance (FAB)**.

Illustrative model:

Approved Foundry investment: **$20,000**

FAB begins at **$20,000**.

While FAB remains outstanding, the current working model allocates **50% of defined eligible recovery revenue** to FAB recovery, capped at the remaining balance. The other portion remains operating revenue under the applicable project/company accounting rules.

Example:

Eligible recovery revenue: $4,000

Recovery allocation: $2,000

Operating allocation: $2,000

New FAB: $18,000.

When FAB reaches $0, the recovery layer ends and the approved post-recovery residual formula becomes eligible.

This is currently a model/ledger doctrine, not automatic money movement. Eligible revenue definitions, tax treatment, accounting, securities implications, employment treatment, and project agreements require professional review before live financial execution.

# 10. Ownership, Equity and Residual Economics

Company ownership and project residual participation are different instruments and should not be casually mixed.

## Company Equity
Company shares represent ownership of Company Foundry itself. Equity should be scarce, governed, documented, subject to vesting/repurchase rules where appropriate, and issued only after securities/tax/legal review.

A builder completing a project phase does not automatically become a co-owner of the company unless the applicable compensation agreement specifically grants equity.

## Project Residuals
A project can separately allocate a defined portion of post-recovery project economics among Company Foundry, a Capstone/project lead, and contributing team members.

Current Capstone doctrine:

- Company Foundry retains **at least 25%** of defined residual economics.
- A Capstone lead may receive **up to 51% of residual economics**, not 51% ownership of the company or project entity.
- Team members can receive defined residual participation.
- All allocations must total 100% of the defined residual pool.

Illustrative post-recovery allocation:

Company Foundry: 30%

Capstone Lead: 40%

Team: 30%

The exact formula can vary by project.

## Phase Compensation
The Foundry intends to make each approved build phase a unit of economic value. Depending on company liquidity, talent needs, project economics, and dilution limits, a phase could potentially be compensated through one or more approved instruments:

- cash project payment
- hourly training wages during the Academy period
- project residual participation
- company equity or equity-linked award where legally structured
- milestone/bonus payment
- combinations of the above.

The phase's value should be determined before assignment where practical through a governed phase-value model rather than negotiated emotionally after completion.

## Equity Scale
Whether the corporation authorizes 100, 1,000,000, or another number of shares is an administrative capitalization decision; percentage ownership is what matters economically. A larger authorized share count can permit finer-grained awards, but does not itself create more company value.

# 11. Recruitment and Team Expansion

The Foundry model encourages successful builders and project leads to recruit additional talent into the system. New recruits do not bypass Academy, quality, legal, or production controls simply because an existing member referred them.

The long-term model can create teams around products, with the Capstone/project lead helping develop a capable production group while Company Foundry supplies training infrastructure, AI/tooling, governance, capital where approved, shared services, commercial support, and portfolio-level leverage.

The objective is potentially very large team capacity without abandoning quality control or creating uncontrolled ownership dilution.

# 14. November 2026 Commercial Launch Doctrine

The recommended launch posture is intentionally focused:

**One flagship commercial product.**

**One paid implementation/pilot offer.**

**One controlled Cohort 1.**

The company should resist launching ten products simply because it can build ten products.

The earliest cash engine is expected to be founder-led institutional implementation and pilots rather than low-price subscriptions alone.

Illustration:

Three $10,000 institutional implementations = $30,000 revenue.

At $39/month, approximately 64 customers retained for an entire year would be required to generate roughly the same $30,000.

Therefore early commercial effort should prioritize customers with urgent problems and enough value creation to support meaningful contracts.

# 16. Market Position

Company Foundry should avoid competing as merely another 'AI agency' or 'coding bootcamp.'

Its intended category is closer to an **AI-native venture and production foundry**.

Differentiators include:

- substantial starting IP rather than an empty startup thesis
- founder-developed build methodology
- beginner-to-production training system
- real production work inside training
- mandatory Capstone engineering and defense
- Master Build Plan discipline
- multiple AI systems used intentionally rather than a single chatbot workflow
- evidence-based acceptance rather than trusting AI completion claims
- human controls around consequential decisions
- portfolio economics rather than reliance on one product
- ability to extend the model beyond software into books/research/IP
- residual participation that can align builders with product outcomes
- internal compute infrastructure that can reduce dependence on external services for appropriate workloads over time.

The weakness is equally clear: breadth can destroy focus. The portfolio is an advantage only if Company Foundry rigorously prioritizes products by commercial evidence.

# 17. Primary Risks

## Commercial Focus Risk
The largest immediate risk is building faster than the company sells. Product creation must become subordinate to customer evidence during launch.

## Legal / Employment / Securities Risk
Internship wages, contractor relationships, equity compensation, residual participation, IP assignment, restrictive covenants, revenue sharing, securities, tax, and payroll rules require qualified counsel/accounting review. The company should not rely on aggressive non-compete or post-employment revenue-capture provisions as its primary IP defense. Strong confidentiality, invention/IP assignment, access controls, trade-secret practices, narrowly tailored non-solicitation where lawful, and properly structured economic agreements are more durable foundations.

## Key-Person Risk
The methodology currently depends heavily on founder judgment. The Academy, Master Plan system, QA, and evidence doctrine exist partly to reduce that dependence.

## Quality Risk
Rapid AI-assisted construction can produce impressive but fragile systems. Workstation certification, tests, human review, defect ledgers, and stabilization are mandatory counterweights.

## Portfolio Valuation Risk
Internal effort is not equivalent to market value. Portfolio value should be updated only as completion, ownership clarity, customers, revenue, retention, and market evidence improve.

## Cash Risk
$20,000 is enough to begin carefully, not enough to tolerate undisciplined hiring or months without sales.

# 18. Governance Principles

1. No product is valuable merely because we love it.
2. No AI certifies its own consequential work.
3. No learner automatically receives ownership because they trained with the company.
4. No Capstone automatically receives funding because the learner graduates.
5. Capital contributions and labor contributions are accounted separately.
6. Company equity and project residuals are separate economic instruments.
7. Foundry-funded pilots repay approved advances under documented rules before post-recovery residual formulas activate.
8. Company Foundry retains meaningful participation in the products it incubates.
9. Every major build should have a Master Build Plan.
10. Every phase should have scope, value, acceptance criteria, and evidence.
11. The next build should increasingly be determined by evidence rather than excitement.
12. Customer revenue is stronger validation than internal completion percentages.
13. Legal, tax, accounting, employment, securities, privacy, and compliance review are launch infrastructure—not cleanup work.

# 19. What Success Looks Like

The first proof is not a billion-dollar valuation.

The first proof is:

- a customer pays Company Foundry for something it created;
- a trainee with limited prior development experience learns the method;
- that trainee completes a bounded production phase that passes independent review;
- the resulting product creates additional revenue or enterprise value;
- a credible Capstone enters the pipeline;
- Company Foundry can finance selected growth from internally generated cash rather than continuously requiring outside capital.

If that cycle repeats, Company Foundry becomes more than a development shop. It becomes a compounding production system.

# 22. Founder's Thesis

The fundamental bet behind Company Foundry is not simply that AI makes programmers faster.

The bet is that AI makes **productive technical capability teachable to a much larger group of people**, and that a company which combines that capability with strong product judgment, disciplined engineering, shared infrastructure, capital, ownership incentives, and a portfolio of real intellectual property can create a new kind of organization.

The company does not need every learner to become extraordinary, and it does not need every product to succeed.

It needs a repeatable system that finds capable people, teaches them to build responsibly, places them on economically useful work, identifies the strongest product ideas, finances the best opportunities, preserves meaningful Company participation, and learns faster after every cycle.

If the Foundry can repeatedly do that, its most important asset will eventually be larger than any single application in the current inventory.

**Its most important asset will be the machine that creates the next one.**
