# Company Foundry Master Plan

**Status:** Founder Doctrine v0.1 — discovery and architecture
**Canonical home:** LocalBrain
**Started:** 2026-08-28
**Purpose:** Build a for-profit AI venture studio that converts existing software IP, disciplined AI-assisted development, paid apprenticeship, and shared economic participation into a repeatable company-building system.

> Build people. Build products. Build owners.

## 1. Governing Thesis

The company is not a conventional software agency, coding bootcamp, or single-product startup. It is a venture studio and software foundry designed to repeatedly turn validated problems into commercial software while training new builders on real production projects.

The intended flywheel is:

**Existing IP / validated problem → product audit → master build plan → paid apprentice team → phase acceptance gates → beta → revenue → graduate builders → new product teams → additional products.**

Growth must be described and measured as **recursive scaling**, not assumed infinite growth.

## 2. Founder Doctrine — Initial Decisions

These are working decisions and may be revised through explicit decision records.

1. Existing credible software assets are the starting portfolio; the company will not manufacture projects merely to keep trainees busy.
2. Product selection must be evidence-driven: customer problem, technical maturity, commercial path, cost to beta, moat, regulatory risk, and expected return.
3. Apprentices are paid while learning. Initial working assumption: **$20/hour** during the finite training/internship period, subject to legal, financial, and employment review.
4. Training occurs on real company projects under master build plans with phase-level acceptance criteria.
5. A capstone requires taking a validated product or substantial product unit through a defined build-and-launch process.
6. Graduation into the ownership/economic-participation layer is earned through demonstrated production capacity, judgment, teamwork, reliability, and alignment with company standards.
7. Parent-company equity, product economics, wages, milestone payments, royalties, and residual-profit participation are separate compensation instruments.
8. The parent company should generally retain ownership/control of core software IP. Product leaders may earn substantial economic participation without fragmenting IP ownership.
9. No equity formula, product-profit percentage, non-compete, post-employment revenue claim, or founder allocation is final until legal/tax/accounting review.
10. GitHub + LocalBrain are the durable institutional record. Major decisions must be written, versioned, and auditable.

## 3. Proposed Operating Model

### A. Parent / Venture Studio
Owns or controls core IP, shared infrastructure, brands where appropriate, security standards, AI infrastructure, finance, legal/compliance, product governance, training standards, and portfolio allocation.

### B. Product Ventures
Each commercial product receives its own scorecard, P&L/economic ledger, roadmap, team, phase plan, launch gates, customer hypothesis, pricing hypothesis, and residual pool if authorized.

### C. Builder Academy
A paid apprenticeship system that teaches the company's AI-assisted development method through production work. Progress is based on accepted output and demonstrated capability rather than seat time alone.

### D. Compute / Foundry Infrastructure
A high-capacity GPU server is intended to become shared company infrastructure for local inference, embeddings/RAG, document processing, development agents, testing, experimentation, and workloads where local compute improves economics, privacy, or capability.

## 4. Economic Architecture — Working Model

Do not collapse all compensation into common stock.

Potential instruments:

- Hourly apprentice compensation
- Fixed phase/milestone payment
- Parent-company restricted equity/options or equivalent
- Product-specific economic participation
- Time-limited revenue share or royalty
- Defined distributable-profit pool participation
- Performance bonuses
- Leadership/founder grants subject to vesting

Every phase may eventually carry a **Phase Value Record** defining cash value, company-equity value, product-economic value, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risk, and who approved completion.

### Capstone / Product Leadership Concept

The founder has proposed that a qualifying capstone leader may receive up to **51% of a defined product profit/economic pool** while meeting continuing production requirements. This is **not yet a final ownership rule**. The legal/economic design should preserve parent-company IP and governance while creating unusually meaningful upside for builders who create successful products.

## 7. Founding-Five Hypothesis

Current hypothesis for first commercialization focus:

1. **SousChef** — fastest consumer commercialization candidate.
2. **CampaignOS / RedDirt** — strongest founder-domain moat and immediate real-world proving ground.
3. **LocalBrain** — potentially highest-value enterprise/private-AI platform.
4. **VoteMatch** — narrow, legible customer problem with existing technical depth.
5. **CanonForge** — reusable knowledge-system architecture with creator/professional potential.

**FoundryOS** should initially be evaluated as the internal factory/platform powering the portfolio rather than automatically treated as the first external product.

This hypothesis can change after the portfolio audit.

## 8. Portfolio Audit Standard

Every credible repository/product family will be scored on:

1. Product identity and duplicates/predecessors
2. Current production/deployment state
3. Technical maturity
4. Estimated V1 beta readiness
5. Security/auth/multi-tenancy/billing readiness
6. Customer and problem
7. Existing proof of demand/use
8. Competitive alternatives
9. Differentiation / moat
10. Monetization model and pricing hypothesis
11. Work required to first revenue
12. Infrastructure and GPU opportunity
13. Staffing requirement
14. Legal/regulatory/data risk
15. IP ownership/provenance risk
16. 1-, 3-, and 5-year scenario economics
17. Kill / hold / incubate / accelerate recommendation

No repository count will be treated as a product count. Related repositories will be collapsed into product families.

## 9. Builder Academy — Initial Architecture

Proposed progression:

**Orientation → Method → Guided Production → Independent Phases → Team Production → Capstone → Graduation Decision → Venture Builder / Contractor / Exit.**

Training should create a verifiable production record. Future builder profiles may track accepted phases, deployments, migrations, AI pipelines, tests, production incidents resolved, products launched, leadership performance, and attributable revenue where defensible.

Graduation does not automatically grant ownership. The capstone and total performance determine whether the company invites the builder into longer-term ownership/economic participation.

## 10. Legal / Governance Workstream

Requires qualified counsel before implementation. Topics include:

- Entity and subsidiary/product structure
- Founder agreements
- Securities/equity issuance
- Vesting and repurchase rights
- IP and invention assignment
- Confidentiality and trade secrets
- Contractor vs employee classification
- Wage/hour rules for apprentices/interns
- Non-solicitation and lawful competitive restrictions
- State-specific non-compete enforceability
- Product revenue/profit participation agreements
- Tax treatment of equity and profit interests
- Data/privacy/security obligations by product
- Political/campaign/compliance separation where applicable

**Explicit guardrail:** the proposed claim to 75% of projects sold by a former builder for two years is not adopted doctrine. Counsel must design lawful, narrow protection of company IP, trade secrets, customers, and legitimate business interests.

## 13. Decision Rule

**We do not confuse enthusiasm with validation.**

A product advances because evidence supports it. A builder graduates because accepted work supports it. Equity is issued because a documented agreement supports it. Revenue projections remain scenarios until customers support them.

The ambition is large; the operating system must remain disciplined enough to survive success.
