## Product truth

```txt
You're not building an AI.
You're building your second brain and your operating company.
```

---

## Four modes (how domains serve Steve)

```txt
1. Remember for me     — memory, data, contacts, canon, books
2. Think with me       — CoS, research, planning, decisions
3. Do work with me     — engineering, creative, media, production
4. Run my business with me — CFO, compliance, campaigns, ops, metrics
```

---

## Domain tree

```txt
Steve
 └── Chief of Staff (Executive Domain)
       ├── Engineering Division      — Burt, Git, builds, architecture
       ├── Creative Division         — Novel Studio, writing, voices, canon
       ├── Research Division         — voters, census, GIS, elections, APIs
       ├── Data Division             — platform, Database Studio, ETL
       ├── Finance Division          — CFO, campaign books, household, business
       ├── Operations Division       — campaigns, grants, compliance ops
       ├── Media Division            — social, photography, podcast
       ├── Relationships Division    — contact intelligence, not CRM lists
       ├── Learning Division         — OJT from real work
       └── System Division           — machine, storage, API health
```
