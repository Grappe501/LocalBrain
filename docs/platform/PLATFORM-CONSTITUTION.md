# LocalBrain Platform Constitution

> **Status:** 🔒 **Constitutional essence · complete at reservation** · 2026-07-05  
> **Scope:** Governs **relationships** — people, institutions, knowledge, ownership, collaboration, evidence. Not implementation.  
> **Rule:** Small · durable · difficult to change. **No further articles without constitutional amendment.**

Implementation may change. This should not. AI, campaigns, databases, GitHub, React, OCR, and volunteer management are **implementations** — not named here because they should not be.

Interpretive detail lives in linked architecture docs — not in this document.

---

## Preamble

> **The purpose of this constitution is to preserve human sovereignty, institutional integrity, and organizational learning while enabling governed collaboration. It defines enduring principles, not implementation details.**

The six articles answer timeless questions — relevant regardless of GitHub, AI models, or technologies that do not yet exist:

| Question | Article |
| -------- | ------- |
| Who owns the person? | I — Personal Sovereignty |
| Who owns institutional knowledge? | II — Institutional Sovereignty |
| How do people join institutions? | III — Governed Relationships |
| How is history preserved? | IV — Historical Integrity |
| How do people collaborate? | V — Governed Collaboration |
| How does the platform evolve? | VI — Evidence Before Change |

Most software begins with features, screens, APIs, and databases. This constitution begins with sovereignty, property, provenance, evidence, and governance — a foundation that can outlast individual implementations.

---

## Article I — Personal Sovereignty

**People own themselves.**

Every person has one LocalBrain.  
Their Personal Ledger belongs to them.

---

## Article II — Institutional Sovereignty

**Institutions own their institutional knowledge.**

Institutional assets belong to the institution that received them through governed contribution.

---

## Article III — Governed Relationships

**Sponsorship governs the relationship between people and institutions.**

Sponsorship grants or revokes access.  
It does not transfer ownership.

---

## Article IV — Historical Integrity

**Provenance is permanent.**

Ledgers preserve who contributed what and when.  
Authorship is never rewritten.

---

## Article V — Governed Collaboration

**Collaboration occurs inside bounded workspaces.**

Workspaces preserve the work.  
Personal brains remain sovereign.

---

## Article VI — Evidence Before Change

**Institutions evolve through evidence.**

Architecture serves governance.  
Governance serves operators.  
Operators produce evidence.  
Evidence justifies change.

---

## Amendment discipline

These six articles are **complete** at reservation. The preamble explains *why*; it is not an article and does not expand the law.

Treat this document as mature open-source projects treat governance documents:

| Work type | Edits constitution? |
| --------- | ------------------- |
| Ordinary engineering | **No** |
| Feature requests | **No** |
| Bug fixes | **No** |
| Performance work | **No** |
| Constitutional amendment | **Yes** — explicit process · evidence that current principles fail to govern a real situation |

| Event | Response |
| ----- | -------- |
| Operator evidence confirms principles | Implement specializations — not new articles |
| Operator evidence reveals a **constitutional gap** | Rare · deliberate · amendment-level deliberation |
| Feature request | Implementation beneath this constitution |

**Do not expand the six articles** until reality proves one insufficient. Let operators, evidence, and time determine whether the constitution has earned the right to remain unchanged.
