## Purpose

LocalBrain started as software, became a platform, then a cognitive architecture, then axiomatic law. The next discipline is **how to improve that architecture over time** — grounded in evidence.

This agenda holds questions worth years of investigation. Each should eventually become a formal hypothesis (H-*) with measurement plan.

---

## Foundational questions

### Judgment and improvement

* Can executive judgment be **measurably improved** over months and years — not merely task throughput?
* Which cognitive architectures minimize [Executive Cognitive Load](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_LOAD.md) while maximizing [Judgment Quality](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_METACOGNITION.md)?
* Does optimizing for [tension reduction](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITION.md) outperform classical task-priority scheduling on long-horizon outcomes?

### Memory and knowledge

* What kinds of memory **actually improve decisions**, and which merely consume attention?
* Can [organizational wisdom](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_METACOGNITION.md#wisdom-accumulation) accumulate without accumulating noise?
* Does [Executive Question](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_QUESTION_REGISTRY.md)-organized recall outperform keyword retrieval on MCP and ECL?
* How should [uncertainty propagate](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_EPISTEMOLOGY_CONVENTION.md) through an executive decision system?

### Prediction and models

* Can [Mission Completion Probability](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_INTENT.md) be predicted more accurately than unaided human intuition?
* How accurately can [World Model Accuracy](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#world-model-accuracy) be estimated daily?
* Do [competing hypotheses](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#competing-hypotheses) reduce premature convergence without increasing ECL?

### Agency and initiative

* When does [emergent observation](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_EPISTEMOLOGY_CONVENTION.md) create leverage vs. noise?
* Can [Self-Doubt](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_METACOGNITION.md#self-doubt) thresholds be calibrated to improve JQ without missing critical deadlines?
* Does [falsification search](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#falsification) improve confidence calibration faster than outcome-only learning?

### Coherence and compression

* Can [Executive Coherence](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITION_AXIOMS.md#executive-coherence) detection prevent strategic drift before outcomes degrade?
* Does [cognitive compression](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#cognitive-compression) improve recall efficiency without violating conservation?
* How should [Knowledge Gravity](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITION_AXIOMS.md#knowledge-gravity) weights be learned vs. assigned?

### Science of the platform

* Which hypotheses failed — and what did failures teach about the architecture?
* Does [Knowledge ROI](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#knowledge-roi) ranking change Steve's information diet measurably?
* Are [predictive surprises](./LOCALBRAIN_EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_SCIENCE.md#predictive-surprise) clustered in domains where World Model Accuracy is lowest?
