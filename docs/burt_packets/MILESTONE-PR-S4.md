## Expected pressure areas

- Does the Mission Stack actually reduce executive cognitive load — or add ceremony?
- Do Executive Questions map to real decisions executives face daily?
- Is Chief of Staff synthesis distinguishable from dashboard aggregation?
- Does Conservation of Understanding survive real information overload?
- Can an executive trust recommendations without understanding the full graph?
- What fails when the executive is wrong, tired, or politically constrained?
- Does the architecture respect that executives delegate — not compute?
