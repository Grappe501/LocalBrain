## 6. Institutional memory

**Slice:** `LB-OS-03X-INSTMEM` · **Capability:** `CAP-FUT-INSTMEM-001`

Every important decision eventually answers:

> **Why** did we decide this?

Not just what. Years later:

> Why did we choose SendGrid over Mailgun?  
> Why did we stop using Google Calendar?

Retrieve: original options · assumptions · tradeoffs · who agreed · later outcome · whether the decision aged well.

Organizational memory — not documentation.
