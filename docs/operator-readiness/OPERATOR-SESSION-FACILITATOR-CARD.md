# Operator Session — Facilitator Card

> **Prime Directive:** [**Protect the evidence.**](./PRIME-DIRECTIVE.md)  
> **Audience:** Observer / facilitator — **not** the operator  
> **Print:** One page · keep visible during the session  
> **Walkthrough:** [OPERATOR-WALKTHROUGH-001](./WALKTHROUGH-001-SCENARIO.md) · [Evidence template](./WALKTHROUGH-001-EVIDENCE-TEMPLATE.md)

---

## Objective

**Observe.**

Do not teach. Do not defend the software. Do not explain architecture.

---

## During the session

**Allowed:**

- *"What would you do next?"*
- *"Tell me what you're thinking."*

**Not allowed:**

- Navigation hints
- Terminology explanations
- Architectural explanations
- *"Click here"*
- *"That's expected"*

**If assistance becomes necessary:**

| Time | Reason | Assistance level |
| ---- | ------ | ---------------- |
| | | P0 / P1 |

Continue the session. Log contamination — do not restart unless the operator requests it.

---

## Observe

Capture (scribe writes; facilitator confirms completeness):

- Hesitations
- Recoveries
- Wrong turns
- Confidence
- Questions
- Unexpected success
- Workarounds

**Remember:** Positive evidence is evidence.

---

## After completion

Ask **only after** the walkthrough is finished:

1. What felt easiest?
2. What felt hardest?
3. What surprised you?
4. Where were you least confident?
5. If this were your job tomorrow, what would worry you?
6. What one thing would you change first?

No debate. No defense.

---

## Close

Complete:

- [ ] Evidence template
- [ ] Readiness dimensions
- [ ] OEC references
- [ ] Signatures

**Session complete.** Evidence goes to interpretation and governance — not to live product debate.

## Discipline chain

```text
Evidence  →  Interpretation  →  Recommendation
```

Capture first. Analyze later. See [Evidence-Driven Development](./EVIDENCE-DRIVEN-DEVELOPMENT.md).
