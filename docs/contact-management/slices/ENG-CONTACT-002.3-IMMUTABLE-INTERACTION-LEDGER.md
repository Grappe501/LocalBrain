# ENG-CONTACT-002.3 — Immutable Interaction Ledger

> **Status:** **RESERVED** — not authorized  
> **Parent:** [ENG-CONTACT-002 Charter](../ENG-CONTACT-002-CHARTER.md)  
> **Prerequisite:** Prior 002.x slices **INHERITED**

## Behavioral question (binding)

> **Can the immutable interaction ledger preserve objective institutional history?**

## Scope (indicative)

Append-only ledger of **objectively observed** events: created · email sent · meeting · call · text · volunteer event · donation · conversation · document · photo · campaign · speech · draft · appointment · institutional field edits (who · when · what · why · previous · new).

## Boundary (binding)

```text
Contact Management owns the immutable interaction ledger.
Relationship Studio constructs timelines, stories, and interpretations from that ledger.
```

## Excluded

Trust trajectory · influence · suggested reconnects · semantic “who met whom” queries · RS-owned event store.
