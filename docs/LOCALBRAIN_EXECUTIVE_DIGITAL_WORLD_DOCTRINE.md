## Privacy north star

> LocalBrain protects Steve's privacy by **minimizing disclosure**, **controlling identity exposure**, and making **every external data flow visible and governed**.

Not "go everywhere invisible." Operational framing:

```txt
Minimize what leaves the machine.
Mask unnecessary identity.
Use local models when possible.
Route sensitive work locally.
Send only small, sanitized context to external AI.
Log every external AI disclosure.
```
